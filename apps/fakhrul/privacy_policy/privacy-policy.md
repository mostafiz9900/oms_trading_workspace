# Privacy Policy

**Fakhrul Securities App**  
**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

Md. Fakhrul Islam Securities Limited ("Fakhrul Securities," "we," "us," or
"our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Fakhrul
Securities mobile application (the "App") and its related services collect,
use, store, and share information.

## Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, the App and our brokerage services may
handle the following information:

- **Account and identity information:** BO account ID, investor or client ID,
  username, name, email address, mobile number, and related brokerage account
  details. During registration or verification, we may also process identity
  details already associated with your brokerage account, such as your
  investor name and father's name.
- **Authentication and security information:** login credentials, one-time
  verification codes, authentication tokens, and a device-specific identifier
  used to secure account access and support biometric login.
- **Brokerage and market information:** portfolio and holdings, cash or
  balance information, watchlists, market activity, and orders or transaction
  information needed to provide brokerage features.
- **Device and app information:** Firebase Cloud Messaging device token used
  for push notifications, app and device diagnostics, crash reports, and
  technical logs used to keep the App reliable and secure.
- **Preferences stored on your device:** settings such as theme and recent
  stock searches. Authentication data and other app state may also be stored
  locally to keep you signed in and operate app features.

The App does not receive your fingerprint or face data. If you enable
biometric login, your device's operating system performs the biometric check.
The App uses a device-specific credential for the login feature; the biometric
template remains under the device platform's control.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- verify your brokerage account and authenticate you;
- provide portfolio, market, order, and account services;
- send security, account, service, and market notifications when enabled;
- protect accounts, detect misuse, and maintain the security of our services;
- diagnose crashes, troubleshoot issues, and improve app reliability; and
- meet applicable legal, regulatory, audit, and recordkeeping obligations.

## How information is shared

We share information only as needed to operate the App and brokerage services:

- **Fakhrul Securities systems and authorized service providers** receive
  account, authentication, and brokerage information to verify and service
  your account, process instructions, provide support, and meet legal or
  regulatory obligations.
- **Google Firebase** provides push messaging through Firebase Cloud
  Messaging and crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics. Google may
  process device and app information on our behalf under its service terms.
- **Authorities and market infrastructure** may receive relevant information
  when required to execute or settle transactions, comply with applicable law,
  respond to a valid legal request, or protect users and our services.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with third parties for
their own advertising. The App does not include an advertising SDK in its
current configuration.

## Storage, security, and retention

Some preferences and app state are stored on your device. Account, brokerage,
and transaction information is processed by Fakhrul Securities systems and
may be retained by us and our service providers for as long as needed to
provide services, protect accounts, resolve disputes, and meet legal,
regulatory, audit, and recordkeeping requirements.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect
information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely
secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and account deletion

You can control push notifications through your device settings. You can sign
out of the App and clear locally stored app data through your device settings.

To request deletion of your App account and associated personal data, open
**Contact Us** in the App and email us, or email
[fisbdltd@gmail.com](mailto:fisbdltd@gmail.com) with the subject **Account and
data deletion request**. Include your BO account ID and the email address or
mobile number registered with your account so we can verify your identity. Do
not include your password or a one-time verification code. You may also use
these contact details to ask questions about your information or request a
correction.

We will review and process verified requests within a reasonable time. Closing
your App account may require separate steps for your brokerage account. We may
retain records that we are required to keep for securities, financial,
regulatory, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. We will restrict the use of
retained records to those purposes and delete or anonymize them when the
retention requirement ends.

Please do not email passwords, one-time codes, or other login secrets.

## Children

The App is intended for Fakhrul Securities brokerage customers and is not
designed for children. Brokerage account eligibility is governed by applicable
law and Fakhrul Securities account requirements.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change.
We will update the effective date above when we publish a revised version.
